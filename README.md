# STUDYMATE AI

### Your Personal AI Study Companion

STUDYMATE AI is an AI-powered learning
assistant designed to help students learn
smarter and understand difficult topics.

## Our Goals

- Explain difficult topics simply.
- Help students with homework.
- Generate study notes.
- Create practice quizzes.
- Help students prepare for exams.

## Our Vision

Making learning easier, smarter and
accessible for every student.

Built with passion and innovation.
